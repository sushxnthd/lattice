Independent executable enumeration of all 256 binary target/control outcomes for four variants (base, component A, component B, full). Requiring both single-component target outcomes to fail and baseline target failure leaves **8/256** assignments. This is a finite combinatorial theorem under the specified Boolean model, **not** an empirical estimate of real-world failure frequency. It does not replace frozen prospective qualification or independent production oracles. No real PR qualified and no model inference was run. The 89 reserved issues remain sealed.
