# K-SYNTH-1 Run580: repository concentration sensitivity

Executed exact enumeration of 4096 independent repository sign flips in three deterministic synthetic designs. All have 240 discordant issues and identical 180 IR-only versus 60 Python-only successes. Exact issue-level binomial McNemar p=4.0990161694649685e-15 in all three. Repository-level exact two-sided sign-flip p changes from 0.14599609375 (balanced 9 positive/3 negative), to 0.18408203125 (unequal sizes, 9/3), to 0.7255859375 (one dominant repository, 6/6). Thus even fixed issue-level effect sizes do not determine cluster-level evidence; allocation across repositories matters.

This is a synthetic statistical counterexample, NOT real model evidence. Cluster sign-flip interpretation assumes independent, sign-exchangeable repository effects under the sharp null; unequal clusters and recruitment can complicate that assumption. Prospectively cap per-repository influence, record repo counts, and preregister issue-weighted and repository-weighted estimands plus uncertainty before evaluating models.
