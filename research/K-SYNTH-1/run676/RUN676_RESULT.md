# K-SYNTH-1 Run676: finite-sample independent-repository mean inference

Run675 established that sign-flip permutation is not generally a test of zero mean without sign symmetry. This run supplies a conservative, assumption-explicit alternative: for independent identically distributed repository-level paired effects X in [-1,1], Hoeffding's inequality yields a two-sided 95% confidence interval around the equal-repository sample mean with radius sqrt(2 log(40)/R). The interval is clipped to [-1,1]. This is valid without sign symmetry or within-repository issue independence, provided repository samples are independent and identically distributed. It does not solve selection bias, prospective qualification, model quality, or cross-repository dependence.

Executable exact binomial enumeration uses the Run675 asymmetric zero-mean counterexample, X=+1 with probability 0.1 and X=-1/9 otherwise. Exact coverage for R=12,30,100,200,400 is at least 0.9999999945, and null rejection at R=12 is 5.455e-9, confirming strong conservatism in this example. Radii: R=100 0.27162; R=200 0.19206; R=400 0.13581; R=800 0.09603. Grid tests check >=95% coverage for 20 enumerated (R,p) configurations. Code and raw JSON are in the local reproducibility archive.

Important: independence alone with heterogeneous repository distributions supports concentration about the average of their expectations, not automatically a common-population estimand.
