# Run554: aggregate-control compensation counterexample

Executed exhaustive 2^3 full-arm outcome masks with baseline controls (PASS,PASS,FAIL). A gate requiring the full arm to have at least as many passing controls as baseline incorrectly accepts two of eight masks: (PASS,FAIL,PASS) and (FAIL,PASS,PASS). Both regress a previously passing control while compensating with a previously failing control. Identity-preserving control checks reject both. General count for m baseline-passing and k baseline-failing controls is sum_{lost=1..m} sum_{gained=lost..k} C(m,lost) C(k,gained). This is a finite combinatorial result, not a production repair qualification, and makes no inference about IR performance. The oracle must preserve case identity and compare each frozen nonregression control, not just aggregate totals. All 89 reserved tasks untouched.
