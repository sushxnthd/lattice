# Run770 — bounded wheel release verification

Local CPython test: 4/4 expected decisions. A valid alpha wheel was accepted. Three adversarial wheel fixtures were refused before RECORD decompression: 1 MB highly compressible member (`COMPRESSION_RATIO_LIMIT`), >256 ZIP entries (`ENTRY_COUNT_LIMIT`), and >32 MB aggregate uncompressed size (`TOTAL_UNCOMPRESSED_LIMIT`). This is a proposed resource bound, not an integrated or CI-validated release gate. The Run769 verifier reads ZIP entries without aggregate decompression limits; therefore an internally consistent wheel may exhaust resources even with an externally trusted digest if the digest is trusted for an adversarial wheel. No untrusted wheel was installed or executed. Thresholds are provisional; archive and executable source are held locally pending review. No semantic IR superiority claim.
